Kundan Kumar

AI Safety Research Fellow · PhD in Computer Science · Iowa State University

Email LinkedIn GitHub Substack X (Twitter) YouTube Scholar

I’m an AI researcher with a Ph.D. in Computer Science (minor: Statistics) from Iowa State University, and a current AI Safety Research Fellow at Algoverse.

My path into research started with a practical question: how do we build systems that behave reliably when the real world doesn’t cooperate? That question pulled me from software engineering at Comcast into a PhD focused on safety-critical control for smart energy infrastructure — and eventually toward the broader challenge of making AI systems trustworthy at scale.

I work at the intersection of deep reinforcement learning, AI safety, and physical systems — building agents that not only perform well, but can be trusted in high-stakes environments.

Current Focus

AI Safety Research Fellow @ Algoverse (Jan 2026 – present)
Designing and evaluating methods for agentic AI safety — including behavioral evaluations for reward hacking and deceptive alignment, adversarial robustness frameworks for LLM-based agents, and scalable oversight mechanisms. This builds directly on my doctoral work on physics-informed, safety-critical reinforcement learning.

Research

My doctoral work centered on physics-informed deep reinforcement learning for smart grid control: embedding domain knowledge, safety constraints, and uncertainty directly into the learning process so agents make reliable decisions under distribution shifts and partial observability.

Over time, “how do we keep agents safe?” scaled beyond energy systems. I now focus on AI safety and alignment more broadly:

🔬
Agentic Evaluations
Designing behavioral and mechanistic tests to detect reward hacking, deceptive alignment, and obfuscated internal states in LLM-based agents.
🛡️
Adversarial Robustness
Understanding and hardening AI systems against distributional attacks, prompt injection, and specification gaming in both classical and foundation model pipelines.
🔭
Scalable Oversight
Exploring how humans and increasingly capable AI systems can collaborate to maintain meaningful supervision at scale.
⚡
Safe RL for CPS
Physics-informed DRL with Bayesian uncertainty quantification, constraint satisfaction, and transfer learning for smart energy infrastructure.
🤖
LLM-Integrated Agents
Frameworks connecting perception, planning, and language reasoning for autonomous systems — bridging interpretable decision-making with low-level control.
👁️
Autonomous Perception
Vision-based perception (detection, segmentation, sensor fusion) integrated with learning-based control and trajectory planning.

For a full account of projects and publications, see the Research and Projects pages.
